Privacy policy · September 6, 2026

Their world stays yours.

Little Bloom keeps play history on your iPad. We do not receive your child's profile or play journal.

Who provides Little Bloom

Little Bloom is provided by Daniel Han. For privacy or support questions, contact apps@invincible-ventures.org.

What the app stores

The app stores an optional nickname, a broad age group, play-depth preferences, session length and sound settings. A language preference stays on this device; translations are bundled with the app and work offline. A journal records completed play actions, activity and object identifiers, elapsed play time and session dates. These records create the parent observations and lighthearted keepsakes.

The app keeps the latest 100 saved sessions and an active session draft. Scene arrangements and drawing paths stay in memory during play; raw finger paths are not saved in the journal. Local data is protected using iOS file protection and excluded from device backups.

What we do not collect

There is no Little Bloom account, advertising, analytics or tracking SDK. The app does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location or advertising identifier. We do not receive, sell or share your child's profile or play history. This release does not enable iCloud sync.

Your choices

In the gated parent area, open Settings to export your journal or delete all profile and play data. Export uses the iPad share sheet and sends a copy only to the destination you select. You control exported copies and the services you choose; deleting the in-app journal does not delete those copies.

Deleting the app removes its local journal. Because the journal is excluded from device backups and cloud sync is unavailable in this release, export a copy first if you want to keep it.

Children and play observations

Little Bloom is designed for brief, adult-guided play. Parent settings, export and external links are behind a parental gate. For children under 18 months, the app offers caregiver-led off-screen activities. Parent observations describe app interactions. Shared play keepsakes do not include the nickname or raw event log by default.

Support and external websites

If you email us, we receive your email address and the message you choose to send. We use them to respond to your request. Please do not include your child's identifying or sensitive information. Our email provider processes support correspondence, and we retain it only as needed to address the request and meet applicable obligations.

Links in the parent area open websites you choose to visit. Those sites have their own privacy practices. The hosting provider for this website may process ordinary request information, such as an IP address, to deliver and secure the pages. This site includes no analytics scripts or advertising.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy if the app's data practices change. The date above identifies this version. For a privacy question or request relating to support correspondence, email apps@invincible-ventures.org.
