Help for grown-ups

A little help with Little Bloom.

Ten gentle worlds to explore together, with a journal of your little discoveries.

Contact apps@invincible-ventures.org for help. Tell us your iPad model, iPadOS version, app version and what happened. Please leave out identifying or sensitive information about your child.

Start a session

Tap “Let's play,” answer the grown-up question, and choose an age group and a one-, three-, or five-minute session. Stay beside your little one. Under-18-month selections offer caregiver-led off-screen play.

Choose simpler or richer play

The age group suggests a starting play level. A grown-up can change Play possibilities to offer simpler scenes or additional objects and actions. These levels are play preferences, not a developmental test.

Pause, switch or finish

Tap “Grown-ups” to pause the clock. Solve the parent gate to change activities or choose “Finish & save session.” Moving away from the app also pauses play. Returning requires a grown-up to resume. Scenes remain while you switch activities within a session; they reset for a new session.

No sound?

Turn on Gentle sounds in the parent controls, then check the iPad's volume and silent mode. Optional background music has a separate setting. Begin at a comfortable, low volume.

Save or delete your journal

Open parent Settings → Export journal to keep a copy. The latest 100 saved sessions are stored locally and excluded from device backups. This release does not offer cloud sync. Deleting the app removes its local journal.

Settings → Delete all profile and play data clears the in-app journal. Copies you previously exported remain wherever you saved them.

Keep the iPad in one app

A grown-up can configure Guided Access under iPad Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access. Follow the device's setup instructions. Little Bloom cannot prevent system gestures by itself.

What do the observations mean?

The parent journal describes completed play actions, activities explored and items revisited. Each observation explains the moments behind it. Sessions can vary with mood, the scene and a grown-up's helping hand.

What's included?

All ten activities and parent features are included in the paid download. There are no subscriptions, in-app purchases or advertisements. Little Bloom supports iPadOS 17 and later.
